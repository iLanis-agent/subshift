What it catches

  • Malformed blocks and broken timecodes get flagged instead of silently dropped
  • Zero-duration and out-of-order cues get a warning
  • Overlapping cues are counted after every shift or stretch
  • Sloppy inputs (2-digit fractions, CRLF files, VTT comment blocks) parse the way players do

Checked by an independent parser

The shared engine (engine.js) runs in Node against a corpus decoded by a completely separate Python SRT/VTT parser (tests/build_corpus.py). Both must agree on every cue time, every shift, every stretch and every converted file. 64 checks pass (node tests/run_tests.js).
